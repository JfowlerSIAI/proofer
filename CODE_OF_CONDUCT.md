# Code of conduct

Proofer welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
These standards apply to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and
other spaces maintained for the project.

- Treat people respectfully. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give constructive feedback and make room for questions and disagreement.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or sexually harass others.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, private writing, exported
  proofreading logs or another person's personal information.

Report conduct concerns privately to
the [project contact](docs/privacy-policy.md#contact), with the subject
**Proofer conduct report**. Describe the behavior and provide relevant public
links where possible; share only the information needed to understand it.
Do not post a conduct report as a public issue.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions,
or restrict participation. Reports and moderation are handled on a best-effort
basis, with no guaranteed response time.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For ordinary
bug reports and contributions, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
