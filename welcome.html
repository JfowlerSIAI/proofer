    On this computer, when a sentence has a lot of text before it in the same box (about 300 words or more), Proofer will sometimes skip it and leave it as you typed it.

    When you finish a sentence, Proofer fixes typos in it and the fixed words flash green. Press Esc right away to take a correction back.

    Turning Proofer on allows it to write corrections directly in supported English fields on every site except ones you switch off in the toolbar menu.

    Proofer is on. Try it in a supported English text field.

    Pages that were already open need one refresh before Proofer works in them. Save anything you are typing first.

    Proofer keeps a log on this computer of the sentences it checks — including ones it didn't change — up to 400 characters of the text before each, the site, what Proofer proposed (including corrections it chose not to make), and any edits you make to its corrections. Nothing is sent anywhere. Export log saves it as a file you can choose to send; Clear deletes it. Both are in Proofer's toolbar menu.

    Recommended: pin Proofer to the toolbar. It shows a small mark there when it needs your attention, for example if Ollama isn't running.
